## About me

I'm a Fellow Chartered Certified Accountant (FCCA) with 12 years in financial services.

**Big 4 audit (9 years).** I worked at EY Uganda and Rwanda, PwC Bahamas and EY Cayman, finishing as an Audit Manager at EY Cayman. Across those roles I led audit and review engagements for banks, investment funds, insurance companies and SEC-reporting SPACs, as well as pharmaceutical, telecom and manufacturing companies.

**Tenet Bank, Cayman Islands.** I led operations at Tenet Bank, a CIMA-regulated start-up bank whose clients included Web3 foundations and DAOs. I also ran the finance function for a year and a half until a full-time Financial Controller was hired. In that time I:

- wrote the bank's operations policies and its accounting policies and procedures, and helped build its control framework from scratch
- prepared monthly, quarterly and annual IFRS financial statements for the Outsourced CFO, the Managing Director, internal and external auditors and the regulator
- prepared and filed the bank's monthly and quarterly CIMA returns, including the Minimum Liquidity and Quarterly Prudential Returns, and Basel reporting such as the Locational Banking Statistics
- led the bank through its first external audit, which ended in a clean opinion, with the financial statements filed with CIMA on time
- handled information requests from the outsourced internal auditors
